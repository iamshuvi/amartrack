Service

What we actually do

Three jobs, run every week. You approve the messaging, and we handle everything underneath it.

1.

Build the list

We define who is worth writing to, source contacts that match, and verify every address before it is used. Bad lists are the single biggest cause of campaigns that quietly fail, so nothing unverified goes out.

2.

Write the sequence

Five emails over roughly three weeks, written to sound like one person wrote them to one person. Short, specific, no attachments, no stock pitch. We test one variable at a time and keep what works.

3.

Run the infrastructure

Separate sending domains so your main domain is never at risk, full authentication, mailbox warmup, and weekly deliverability monitoring. When a reply lands, it reaches you the same business day.

About

Who we are

SUMO MI is a small outbound team. The MI stands for marketing infrastructure, because that is the actual product: the domains, mailboxes, deliverability and reply handling that outbound needs in order to work at all. Our operators are in India and our clients are in the United States, which we mention up front because you would find out on the first call anyway.

It works because of the time difference rather than despite it. Lists get built and copy gets written while you are asleep, and our reply desk is staffed through your business hours, so a prospect who writes back at nine in the morning hears from a person before lunch.

We are new. We don't have a wall of client logos yet, and we are not going to borrow anyone else's. What we can do is show you the method in detail, tell you honestly whether outbound fits what you sell, and let the first campaign be the argument.
