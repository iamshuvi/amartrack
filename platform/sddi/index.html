Platform Summary

The Skill Development Digital Infrastructure (SDDI) integrates citizens, trainers, institutions, employers and government agencies through a unified digital public infrastructure. The platform supports end-to-end lifecycle management including registration, training delivery, assessments, certification, placement, monitoring, disaster recovery, and enterprise-grade infrastructure.
