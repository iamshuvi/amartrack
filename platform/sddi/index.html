Platform Summary

The Skill Development Digital Infrastructure (SDDI) provides a unified digital ecosystem connecting citizens, trainers, institutions, employers and government departments through one integrated platform.


The platform manages the complete lifecycle of skill development including enrolment, learning management, assessments, certifications, placement, governance, analytics, disaster recovery and enterprise infrastructure.
