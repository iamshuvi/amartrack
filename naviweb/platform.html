Platform

Infrastructure, with an institutional layer beneath it.

UCI is the continuity and analytics layer. The AMARTRACK institutional platform is the surface that captures the data feeding it. Institutions may adopt either; together they close the loop.

Layer one

UCI — the continuity layer

01

Persistent career identity

A consent-based record that follows the individual across institutions and stages, rather than resetting at each transition.

02

Accreditation automation

IQAC operated as a continuous digitised process. NAAC, NIRF and AISHE submissions built from master datasets already held, removing dependence on external preparation.

03

Industry–academia mapping

Syllabus and credit outcomes mapped against live employer requirements, sector by sector, showing where curriculum and demand diverge.

04

Prescriptive analytics

Career diagnostics, capability graph modelling, trajectory forecasting and role transition probability, from individual to district level.

05

National directory unification

Designed to interoperate with APAAR, ABC, PEN, NAD and UDISE+ rather than duplicate them.

06

Stakeholder dashboards

Institution heads, IQAC coordinators, district officers and the Department each viewing the same verified data at their level of responsibility.

Architecture

Built for public infrastructure obligations.

Cloud-native core

Hybrid-cloud hosting with data redundancy, disaster recovery and elastic compute. Containerised via Docker, orchestrated on Kubernetes (AKS).

Microservices framework

Every module operates as an independent service, allowing modular deployment and minimal downtime during expansion.

API-first design

RESTful APIs enable secure communication with government databases including ABC, NAD, PEN and APAAR. Compatible with Aadhaar and UGC-DEB APIs.

Security

AES-256 end-to-end encryption, role-based access control, zero-trust architecture with MFA for administrators, and regular third-party penetration testing.

Data & analytics

Centralised data lake aggregating structured and unstructured data, with an AI-powered analytics engine for real-time institutional and policy insight.

Access anywhere

Responsive web and mobile built on React.js and Flutter, with progressive web app support for offline access in low-connectivity districts.

Compliance and governance. Aligned with UGC, AICTE, CBSE, ICSE and State Board guidelines. Data governance aligned with India's DPDP Act 2023, GDPR and ISO 27001 standards, with integrated support for NAAC, NIRF and UDISE+ documentation.
