UCI — Unified Career Infrastructure

UCI (Unified Career Infrastructure), powered by AMARTRACK, is a lifelong career data infrastructure connecting education, skill development, employment and verification ecosystems through trusted, portable and longitudinal individual career records.

Designed as a foundational population-scale career data infrastructure aligned to the future evolution of digital public infrastructure ecosystems.

Why UCI Exists

Fragmented Career Data

Education, skill and employment data today exists in disconnected institutional silos.

No Lifelong Record

Individuals lack a portable and trusted longitudinal career identity system.

Verification Inefficiency

Employers, institutions and governments spend enormous effort validating credentials repeatedly.

UCI Infrastructure Stack

Career Identity Layer

Persistent lifelong individual career identity across institutions and economic ecosystems.

Credential Graph

Structured linking of education, skills, certifications, experience and achievements.

Trust & Verification Rail

Real-time multi-institution credential validation and trusted data exchange pipelines.

National Infrastructure Potential

UCI is designed to evolve as a foundational career data infrastructure layer interoperable with national digital public infrastructure frameworks, enabling trusted career mobility, workforce intelligence and evidence-based policy systems.



By connecting institutions, individuals and economic systems, UCI enables longitudinal workforce intelligence and career mobility visibility at population scale.
