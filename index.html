UCI - Unified Career Infrastructure

A longitudinal career data infrastructure layer connecting education, skills, opportunity, cross-functional employment, verification ecosystems across institutional and economic systems.

Designed to evolve as a population-scale career infrastructure aligned with future digital public infrastructure evolution.

UCI is not a software product. It is a foundational infrastructure layer enabling career data continuity across institutions, economies and national workforce intelligence systems.

Why Career Infrastructure Must Evolve

Fragmented Career Data

Career records exist across disconnected institutional silos.

No Longitudinal Identity

Individuals lack persistent career identity across systems.

Verification Inefficiency

Institutions repeatedly validate credentials across ecosystems.

Career Data Flow Architecture

Institutions - Schools > Universities > Skill Bodies
UCI Identity Layer - Persistent Career Continuity
Economic Systems - Employers | Industry | Government

National Infrastructure Potential

UCI is designed to evolve as a foundational career data infrastructure interoperable with national digital public infrastructure ecosystems, enabling workforce intelligence and career mobility visibility at population scale.
